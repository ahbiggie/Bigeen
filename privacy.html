In short

  • We collect only what you choose to send us: through the Business Diagnostic, by email or on WhatsApp.
  • This website uses no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.
  • We use your information to review your enquiry and respond to you. We do not sell it.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time.

Who we are

This website is operated by Bigeen Solutions Ltd (“Bigeen”, “we”, “us”), a private company limited by shares, incorporated in Nigeria under the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020, company registration number RC 9484602. Registered office: 23 Ebosele Crescent, Dutse Alhaji, FCT, Nigeria.

Bigeen is the data controller for personal information collected through this website. This notice is written to meet the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

For any question about this notice or your information, email info@bigeensolutions.com.

What we collect

When you complete the Business Diagnostic

About the organisation
Name, website, organisation type, industry or sector, and approximate size.
About you
Your name, role or position, work email, phone number (optional) and preferred contact method.
Your answers
What you are trying to achieve, the challenges you describe, their impact, what has already been tried, what success would look like, where you are in addressing it, and anything else you add.

Your answers stay in the page until you submit. Nothing is saved in your browser, and refreshing or leaving the page clears them.

The Diagnostic asks about your organisation, not about other individuals. Please do not include health information or other sensitive details about identifiable people in your answers.

When you email us or message us on WhatsApp

The contents of your message and the contact details that come with it, such as your email address or phone number.

When you visit the website

Our hosting provider records standard technical information for each request (IP address, browser type, page requested and time) to deliver the site and keep it secure.

What we do not collect

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or social media tracking. Fonts and images are served from our own site, not from third parties.

How we use it

  • To review your Diagnostic and respond to you.
  • To reply to emails and messages.
  • To prepare for a possible engagement, if you ask us to.
  • To operate the website and protect it from misuse.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making, and we do not add you to a marketing list.

Legal basis

Consent
You confirm in the Diagnostic that Bigeen may use your information to review it and contact you about it. You can withdraw that consent at any time by emailing us; this does not affect anything done before you withdrew it.
Steps before a contract
Where you ask us to discuss or propose an engagement.
Legitimate interests
Responding to enquiries you send us, and keeping the website secure.

Who receives it

We use a small number of service providers to run the website and receive enquiries:

Formspree
Receives Business Diagnostic submissions, stores them on Bigeen’s account and forwards them to Bigeen by email.
Vercel
Hosts this website and keeps the technical request logs described above.
Namecheap (Private Email)
Hosts Bigeen’s bigeensolutions.com mailboxes, and stores the emails we send and receive.
WhatsApp
If you choose to message us on WhatsApp, that conversation is also handled under WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy.

These providers process information on our behalf and only for these purposes. We do not sell or rent personal information. We may disclose information where the law requires it.

International transfers

Formspree, Vercel and Namecheap are based in the United States, so information you send through this website may be processed outside Nigeria. Where that happens, we rely on the transfer safeguards the NDPA provides for.

How long we keep it

Diagnostic submissions and enquiries
Deleted within 12 months of our last contact with you, unless they lead to an engagement. This includes the copies held by Formspree and in our email.
Engagements
Where an enquiry leads to work together, we keep the relevant information for as long as the engagement agreement and our legal obligations require.
Technical logs
Kept by Vercel under its own retention periods.

You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

Security

The website is served over HTTPS, and Diagnostic submissions are sent over an encrypted connection. Access to what you send us is limited to the people at Bigeen who need it to respond. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Your rights

Under the NDPA you can ask us to:

  • confirm whether we hold information about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • delete your information;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • provide your information in a portable format;
  • stop using it where you have withdrawn consent.

To make a request, email info@bigeensolutions.com. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we will respond within the time the law requires.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

Children

This website is intended for organisations and the adults who represent them. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we handle personal information, for example by adding a service provider, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Email info@bigeensolutions.com with any question about this notice. See also our Terms of Use.
